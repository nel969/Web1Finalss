BIOGRAPHY

Early Life and Education

Birthplace: Cagayan de Oro City, Philippines. Background: Born into a musical family, both parents were singers, sparking his interest in music. Education: He attended Xavier University — Ateneo de Cagayan, where he majored in Psychology and was part of the glee club.

Career and Breakthrough

Early Beginnings: Before his breakthrough, Nery worked as an English teacher for Japanese students and as a wedding singer. Signing: He was discovered by Kean Cipriano, frontman of Callalily, after a performance in Bukidnon and signed with O/C Records in 2019. Debut: He released his debut album, Letters Never Sent, in 2019. Rise to Fame: He achieved massive success in 2021 with the hit single "Pagsamo," which cemented his place in the OPM scene. Musical Style: Known for his smooth, velvet vocals and open-hearted, emotional songwriting.

Notable Works

Singles/Hits: "Pagsamo," "Binhi," "Higa," "Isa Lang," "Happy w u," and "Pelíkula". Album: Letters Never Sent (2019). Latest Work: "Ayaw Baya" (his first Bisaya single).

About Us

Arthur Nery (born January 28, 1997) is a celebrated Filipino singer-songwriter and record producer, known for his smooth vocals and emotive R&B/neo-soul hits like "Pagsamo," "Higa," and "Binhi". Rising from Cagayan de Oro to become a top OPM artist, his music blends sincere storytelling with modern soul, earning massive streaming records.
